# Browsing a large library

A library with hundreds of game systems, a hundred map creators, or thousands of
tracks and tokens is still quick to get around. Every browse page - the library,
a system collection, maps, tokens, audio and 3D models - gives you the same four
tools:

- the **letter rail**, to jump straight to a letter
- **folder pages**, to open one folder on its own and walk the tree
- **pins**, to keep the places you return to one click away
- an **address for every view**, so a filtered page or a folder can be bookmarked

And on any of these pages, press <kbd>/</kbd> to jump to its search box.

## The letter rail

Once a page lists more than a handful of entries, a slim A-Z rail appears down
the right-hand side. The list itself stays one unbroken scroll - nothing is
split up under letter headings. Only letters that hold something are listed on
the rail, and the letter you have scrolled to is filled in.

- **Click a letter** to jump to it. The list scrolls to that letter's first entry
  and focus moves to it, so the keyboard and screen readers carry on from there.
- **Hover over a letter** to see how many entries it holds and the first few
  names - "T · 9 folders · 1,012 maps: Tehox Maps, Tom Cartos, ...".
- **On a phone or tablet**, tap a letter, or drag your finger along the rail and
  lift it to jump where it is.

Names file under the letter they read as, whatever the accents: "Mörk Borg" is
under M and "Élan" under E. Names starting with a digit or a symbol share `#`.

When the window is too short for a tab per letter, quiet neighbouring letters
merge into a range - `U–Z` - so every tab stays big enough to hit.

The rail follows the sort order:

| Sorted by | The rail jumps by |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Name | First letter |
| Year (systems) | Decade |
| Date added (galleries, ungrouped) | Month |
| Size, page count, duration, track | - no rail, since there is nothing to jump between |

### With the keyboard

The rail is a single stop when you <kbd>Tab</kbd> through the page.

| Key | Does |
|-----|------|
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move between letters |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> <kbd>End</kbd> | First and last letter |
| A letter or digit | Move to that letter |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Jump |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close the preview |

### Ungrouped galleries

Turn **Group** off in a gallery and it becomes one long list. The rail then
jumps by asking the server where each letter starts, and opens the list right
there instead of loading everything before it. A **Show earlier items** button
at the top of the list loads what comes before, a page at a time.

## Folder pages

Each folder in a gallery has an **Open** button beside its name. It opens the
folder on a page of its own, showing the folders inside it - each of which can be
opened in turn - and any files kept directly in it.

A path bar above the folder's name shows where you are: **Maps › Tom Cartos ›
Caverns**. Click any step to go back up to it. The **▾** beside a step lists the
folders next to it, with a filter box when there are many, so you can step
sideways - from *Caverns* to *Crypts* - without going back up first.

Folder pages have their own address, so the browser's Back button, bookmarks and
shared links all work.

## Pins

Pin the folders and systems you go back to all the time. Pins show as a strip at
the top of the page, and each one opens with a click.

- **In a gallery**, press **Pin** beside a folder's name, or beside the name of
  an open folder page.
- **In the library**, press **Pin** at the top of a system's page, or of a
  collection's page.

Click the **×** on a pin in the strip to unpin it. Pins belong to your account,
so they follow you to other browsers and devices. Each view keeps its own pins,
up to 100.

## Addresses you can share

The page's address keeps up with what you are looking at: the open folder, the
sort order, the search, and the filters - for example
`/maps?folder=Tom+Cartos%2FCaverns&sort=added_at&order=desc`. Reload the page,
bookmark it, or send the link to another player, and it opens the same way.

A link that names its own sort or filters opens exactly as written. Otherwise a
page opens with your default saved filter (**Saved filters** on the page's
toolbar), as before.
